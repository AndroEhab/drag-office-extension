2. Description of service

Drag to Sheets is a free Chrome extension that lets you drag and drop spreadsheet files (CSV, TSV, XLSX, XLS) into a browser side panel, clean and preview the data locally, and optionally send the result to your own Google account as a Google Sheets spreadsheet via the official Google Sheets and Drive APIs.
