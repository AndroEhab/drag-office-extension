Drag to Sheets

Drag spreadsheet files into a Chrome side panel, clean them up, and open them in Google Sheets — all without uploading your data until you're ready.

Supported file types

  • CSV .csv
  • TSV .tsv
  • Excel .xlsx & .xls

How it works

File parsing, previewing, cleaning, and merging all happen locally on your device. Your data never leaves your computer unless you take action.

When you click Open in Sheets, the extension sends your data to Google via the official Sheets and Drive APIs to create a spreadsheet in your Google account. No data is ever sent to our servers.

Built-in cleaning tools

  • Trim whitespace
  • Remove empty rows and columns
  • Remove duplicate rows
  • Fix number formatting
  • Normalize dates
  • Normalize headers

Privacy

Your data stays private. No analytics, telemetry, or tracking code is included. See the privacy policy for full details.
