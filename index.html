How to use Drag to Sheets

Clean spreadsheet files before Google Sheets.

Drop a CSV, TSV, XLSX, or XLS file into the Chrome side panel, review the cleanup, then open a clean result in Google Sheets.

01 / IMPORT

Bring in any spreadsheet

Drop files into the Chrome side panel, browse from your computer, or import from a URL.

  • CSV
  • TSV
  • XLSX
  • XLS
02 / CLEAN

Fix the messy middle

Apply the cleanup you need before anything reaches Google Sheets, with a preview of the result.

  • Whitespace
  • Empty rows and columns
  • Duplicates
  • Numbers and dates
  • Headers
03 / MERGE

Make files line up

Open files separately or merge them into one sheet with smart header matching and custom column mapping.

  • Smart mapping
  • Custom mapping
  • Worksheet selection
04 / OPEN

Send the finished data

Open a clean result in your Google account through the official Sheets and Drive APIs. See the privacy policy for data handling.

  • Separate sheets
  • One merged sheet
  • Local-first processing
